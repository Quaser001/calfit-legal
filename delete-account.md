# Delete your CalFit data

CalFit has no accounts. Your profile is tied to a random identifier the app keeps on your phone, not to your name, email or phone number.

## In the app (immediate)
Open CalFit → **Settings** → **Delete my data** → confirm. This removes, at once, from our servers:
- your profile and body details;
- every scan, scan photo and saved serving size;
- every "Was this result right?" answer;
- past days' scan and prescription counts;
- the Google backup link, if you made one.

Each family profile on a phone is deleted separately: switch to it on Home, then delete.

## Without the app
Write to Asefustm@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my CalFit data". Because there is no account, we will ask you to show the request comes from the phone holding that profile. We reply within 30 days. If the app is already uninstalled, its identifier is gone from the phone and the data can no longer be linked to you.

## What we keep
- **Record of your consent** (which notice version you agreed to, and when; no health data), marked withdrawn, as proof that what we did before was with your consent.
- **Today's scan count** (a number and a date), so the daily limit still applies.
- **Server logs** for 1 year, as India's DPDP Rules require. They hold no health data and not your identifier.
